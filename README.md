# Market Campaign Analysis

# About The Project

This dashboard focused on analyzing the performance of a multi-channel digital marketing campaign across five platforms:

i. Google Ads

ii. Facebook

iii. Instagram

iv. Twitter

v. Email

The objective was to evaluate campaign effectiveness by making use of  core digital marketing KPIs including traffic generation, engagement, conversion performance, and return on investment.


# Tool Used: 
a. Power BI
b. Excel


# The Focus: 
Digital Marketing Analytics & ROI Optimization


# Key Performance Indicators 

i. Top Panel Analysis

1. Total Clicks: 1M

It indicates a strong traffic acquisition across platforms. The campaign successfully drove large-scale audience engagement.


2. CTR (Click-Through Rate): 4.29%

A CTR above 4% suggests an effective ad creatives with targets on strategy. This reflects to a strong audience alignment with campaign messaging.


3. ROI (Return on Investment): 282.71%

This is a majorly known for  strength.  For every $1 spent, the campaign generated nearly $2.83 in return which indicates a high financial efficiency.


4. CVR (Conversion Rate): 23%

 A 23% conversion rate is significantly strong. It also suggested that the target audience segmentation was effective and the purchase intent was on a high rate.


ii. Platform Performance Analysis

a. Total Impressions by Platform

	
- Google Ads: ~5.3M

-	Facebook: ~5.2M

-	Instagram: ~5.0M

-	Email: ~4.8M

-	Twitter: ~4.8M

The Insight:

Google Ads and Facebook lead in visibility reach.

Strength:

There was a strong awareness generated across search and social platforms.

Weakness:

Twitter and Email show slightly lower impressions, which may indicate an imbalance budget allocation and poor audience targeting reach.


b. Total Clicks by Platform

Clicks are relatively balanced across platforms (~0.20M–0.23M range).

The Insight:

Traffic acquisition was effectively distributed across. 

Strength:

There was no heavy dependency on a single traffic source.

Weakness:

Balanced clicks do not necessarily mean balanced profitability.  It may require a intense cost-per-click analysis.


c. Total Conversion by Platform

The Google Ads leads in conversions (~53K)  followed by Instagram and Facebook. The Twitter and Email  are lower performers.

The Insight:The search intent platforms (Google Ads) convert better than passive platforms.

Strength:

Google Ads is known to be a strong high-intent conversion driver.

Weakness:

Email and Twitter show weaker conversion output. The potential optimization opportunity were identified in Email personalization and Audience targeting refinement


d. Total Revenue & ROI by Platform

Google Ads generates the highest revenue (~$2.23M), followed closely by Facebook and Instagram.

The Insight:

The Revenue aligns with the leardership of conversion.

Strength:

Campaign spend allocation appears efficient towards the high-performing channels.

Weakness:

If ROI variance exists between platforms and budget re-allocation. It could further optimize profitability.


# The Overall  Market Campaign Strengths

i. Strong ROI (282%) indicates excellent financial efficiency.

ii. High conversion rate (23%) shows effective funnel optimization.

iii. Clear visibility into impressions, clicks, revenue, and conversions.

iv. Balanced multi-channel presence reduces channel dependency risk.

This  dashboard reflects to a data-driven marketing strategy with a strong execution.
